# Site Copy & Content Guidelines

_For all copy on the Accredited Home Healthcare Directory (AHHD) site — UI text, page content, error/empty states, disclaimers._

## Tone

- **Approachable, not clinical.** Users are often overwhelmed — searching for care for themselves or a family member, frequently under stress or time pressure. Copy should bring clarity, not add to the load.
- **Objective and factual.** Don't editorialize, oversell, or use marketing hype ("best," "top-rated," "amazing"). State what's true and let it speak for itself.
- **Supportive and warm, without being saccharine.** Acknowledge the situation is hard without being maudlin about it. Plain, human sentences over corporate or clinical phrasing.

## Plain language

Break down medical and industry terminology into words anyone would understand, the first time it's used on a page. Don't assume the visitor knows accreditation, healthcare, or insurance jargon.

- Explain acronyms on first use (e.g., "ACHC (Accreditation Commission for Health Care)").
- Prefer everyday words over clinical ones where they mean the same thing.
- If a term genuinely needs to stay technical (e.g., "hospice," "home health"), give a one-line plain-language explanation nearby rather than assuming prior knowledge.

## Disclaimers

Disclaimers should be honest about the limits of what we know, without being alarmist. See the finalized website-search-link disclaimer in `MVP-Rollout-RRoadmap.md` as the template for tone: state plainly what we're providing, note that details can change, and point the visitor toward confirming directly — framed as helpful, not as a legal hedge.

## What to avoid

- Marketing superlatives or sales language.
- Unexplained jargon (medical, legal, or accreditation-industry terms).
- Anything that implies a guarantee about a provider's quality of care — we're a directory surfacing accreditation status, not a rating or endorsement service.
